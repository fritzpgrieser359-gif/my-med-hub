Add a lab report

Got results from Labcorp, Quest, or your doctor as a PDF? In the Labcorp app, tap Share and pick My Med Hub, or save the PDF and pick it here. The whole report and the original are saved, and your blood counts go on your chart automatically.

How do I save the PDF to my phone?

If it came in an email (Gmail)

  1. Open the email and scroll down to the PDF attachment at the bottom.
  2. Tap the download arrow on the attachment (not the attachment itself). It saves to your Downloads folder.
  3. Come back here, tap Choose lab report PDF, tap Files, and pick it from Downloads.

If it's in the Labcorp app

  1. Open the result and tap the download or share icon.
  2. If you see Download, tap it and it saves to Downloads. If you only see a share list, choose Save to Files (or Files by Google) and pick Downloads.
  3. Come back here and pick the file the same way.

No PDF, just text in the email? Copy the text and paste it in Add from email below.

Add from email (paste the text)

If something only ever reaches you by email — a result, a doctor's after-visit note, a report — paste the text here instead of retyping it. It's filed with today's date into the right place automatically.
